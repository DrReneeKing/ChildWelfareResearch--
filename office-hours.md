## Book a time

No separate booking link — schedule directly through the **Zoom** item in the Canvas course menu. Students select an available slot there; no Outlook/Teams booking system is used for this course.

## Format

- **Wednesdays, 1:00pm–4:00pm — In-person:** Thayer Hall, Room 317
- **Thursdays, 9:00am–12:00pm — Virtual (Zoom):** Meeting ID **465 836 7382** — join at [zoom.us/j/4658367382](https://zoom.us/j/4658367382). No passcode; **waiting room enabled** — you'll be admitted individually, so join a few minutes early during busy weeks.

Students may choose either day/format when booking, unless a specific week is designated otherwise (e.g., during Phase 2 rotation weeks when the Thursday virtual slot may be more practical than an in-person visit).

## Response time

Email response time: **within 48 hours on weekdays.** This is a slightly wider window than a same-day expectation, intentionally — Phase 2 rotation coordination (site issues, supervisor communication) adds email volume during Weeks 8–13, and this gives room to handle both routine and rotation-related messages without over-promising a faster turnaround than is realistic.

**Time-sensitive exception:** if a message concerns a rotation-site emergency, a Benchmark Checkpoint concern close to the Week 7 deadline, or a Field Readiness concern close to Week 15, say so directly in the subject line — these warrant a faster response than the standard 48-hour window and should be flagged as urgent rather than assumed.

## What to bring

- A specific question or topic, not just "I have questions" — helps make the most of a short slot
- Any assignment draft you want feedback on (Engagement Simulation self-assessment, First Client Case materials, Afrocentric Perspective Case Study draft, Portfolio sections)
- Your student ID or 900# if the conversation touches registration, accommodations, or records

## Group vs. individual bookings

First Client Case groups (Module 4) and rotation-site logistics questions (Modules 5–7) are welcome to book as a group rather than each member booking separately — note this when scheduling.
