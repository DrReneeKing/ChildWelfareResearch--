# Module 5: Implementation I — Child Welfare & Family Systems

**Weeks:** 8–9 · **Phase:** 2 — Implementation (agency rotations) · **GIM Stage:** Implementation

**Overview:** The first of three Phase 2 rotations. Students begin supervised observation at a child welfare/family-services agency site, paired with content on assessing and working with families in diverse cultural contexts. Comp 8 (Intervene) is *introduced* here at exposure level — mastery is CUSW 404's responsibility, not this course's.

**Reading:** Hepworth et al. — Ch. 10 (Assessing Family Functioning in Diverse Family and Cultural Contexts), Ch. 16 (Enhancing Couple and Family Relationships)

**Slides:** [link — TBD]

## Week 8 — Family Systems Content; Rotation Begins
- Assessing family functioning across diverse family structures — not defaulting to a nuclear-family template (directly relevant to the Sullivan–Reid, Torres-Bell, and Washington case vignettes from Module 4)
- Rotation orientation: professional conduct expectations, confidentiality obligations at the agency site, observation-log format
- Rotation begins: 4 hrs/week at assigned child welfare/family-services site

## Week 9 — Rotation Continues
- Enhancing couple and family relationships — intervention-level content (exposure only; full intervention skill-building is CUSW 404's scope)
- Rotation continues
- **Due:** Observation log + reflection

## Assignment: Observation Log + Reflection

Students complete a structured observation log during their rotation hours (what was observed, professional conduct notes, questions raised — no client-identifying detail recorded) and a reflection connecting the rotation experience to the week's readings and to their own First Client Case (Module 4) — how does the real agency setting compare to or complicate the case-study version of assessment and planning?
