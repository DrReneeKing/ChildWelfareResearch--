# Module 05: Diverse Populations II & Vulnerable Groups

**Weeks:** 10–12 (Fri 10/9/26, Fri 10/16/26, Fri 10/23/26)

## Overview

This module continues the population-specific sequence begun in Module 04, examining Native and immigrant children and families, then turns to two of the most vulnerable groups in the child welfare system: victims of trafficking and children experiencing psychological maltreatment.

## Learning Outcome

Describe the distinct child welfare experiences and systemic barriers facing Native and immigrant children and families; explain the intervention process for cases involving trafficking, and identify signs of psychological maltreatment.

## Week 10 (10/9/26) — Native Children and Families; Sexual Abuse

- Native children and families in the child welfare system: tribal sovereignty considerations, the Indian Child Welfare Act (ICWA), and jurisdictional complexity
- The sexual abuse of children: recognition, disclosure patterns, and trauma-informed response
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 10):** 5-minute Tableau beginner tour — conceptual only, no hands-on yet. *Maps to the Pre/Post-Survey's Tableau-beginner-skills item.*

## Week 11 (10/16/26) — Immigrant Children and Families; Psychological Maltreatment

- Immigrant children and families in the child welfare system: language access, immigration status concerns, and culturally responsive practice
- Psychological maltreatment: definitions, recognition challenges (it's often invisible compared to physical indicators), and long-term impact
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 11):** low-stakes RStudio/Posit Cloud access check-in — confirm accounts still work ahead of Module 07's hands-on stretch. If any syntax feels rusty, the [R & RStudio Reference Guide](../resources/r-rstudio-reference-guide.html) is a quick refresher. *Reinforces the RStudio-environment item rather than introducing something new.*

## Week 12 (10/23/26) — Human Trafficking; Intervention Process

- Victims of human trafficking and the child welfare system's role in identification and response
- The intervention process end-to-end: reporting, investigation, and case management — tying back to Module 01's mandated reporting content
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 12):** explore one real AFCARS variable and write a one-paragraph reflection on what it might reveal about a case like the ones discussed this module. *Maps to the Pre/Post-Survey's Data Curation awareness item.*
