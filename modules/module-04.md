# Module 04: Family Engagement & Diverse Populations I

**Weeks:** 8–9 (Fri 9/25/26 & Fri 10/2/26)

## Overview

With foundational content and the data-science launch behind them, students now move into applied content: engaging families directly, and beginning a sequence of modules examining how child welfare experiences differ across populations — starting with the neglect case study and the experiences of domestic children of color in the system.

## Learning Outcome

Apply multidimensional engagement strategies with families in child welfare settings; describe how racial and cultural context shapes child welfare experiences for children of color.

## Week 8 (9/25/26) — Multidimensional Engagement of Families; Neglect Case Study

- Multidimensional family engagement: building trust and rapport across cultural, linguistic, and socioeconomic differences
- Case study analysis: the neglect of children — recognizing patterns, distinguishing neglect from poverty-related hardship
- Breakout session discussion: "What do you see?" — applying engagement principles to the case study
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 8):** 5-minute reading — what "tidyverse" means and why R for Data Science is organized around it. No coding; doesn't compete with Case Study prep. See [tidyverse Core Verbs](../resources/r-rstudio-reference-guide.html#tidyverse) in the reference guide for the six verbs by name. *Maps to the Pre/Post-Survey's tidyverse-familiarity item.*

## Week 9 (10/2/26) — Domestic Children of Color; Physical Abuse

- Disproportionality and disparity in child welfare involvement for domestic children of color
- The physical abuse of children: recognition, documentation, and response
- Connecting historical policy (Module 02) to present-day racial disparities in system involvement
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 9):** 5-minute framing — what exploratory data analysis (EDA) is, conceptually, ahead of the hands-on wrangling stretch in Module 07. No coding. *Maps to the Pre/Post-Survey's EDA-awareness item.*

## Data Science Tie-In

No *coding* activity this module — the sequence stays deliberately light here so the Case Study (Mid-Term) gets full attention. But two 5-minute, no-prep DS Touchpoints (tidyverse in Week 8, EDA framing in Week 9) keep the weekly scaffold continuous rather than leaving a two-week gap in the Pre/Post-Survey's measurable thread. Students should still be maintaining their Data Science 101 skills; DSSWE Module 1 activities resume in Module 06.
