# Module 4: Assessment & Planning

**Weeks:** 5–7 · **Phase:** 1 — Engagement, Assessment & Planning · **GIM Stage:** Assessment → Planning

**Overview:** This module moves from Engagement (Module 3) into the Assessment and Planning stages of the Generalist Intervention Model. Students learn multidimensional assessment, environmental-systems mapping, and collaborative goal contracting — then apply all three through **First Client Case**, a small-group case study sustained across the three weeks. Module 4 closes Phase 1 with the **Benchmark Checkpoint**, the readiness gate students must clear to advance to Phase 2's agency rotations.

**Reading:** Hepworth et al., *Direct Social Work Practice*, 11th ed. — Ch. 8 (Multidimensional Assessment), Ch. 9 (Assessing Intrapersonal and Environmental Systems), Ch. 12 (Negotiating Goals and Formulating a Contract)

## Weekly breakdown

### Week 5 — Multidimensional Assessment; First Client Case launch
- Multidimensional (biopsychosocial-spiritual), strengths-based assessment
- Genograms as an assessment tool: family structure and intergenerational patterns
- Small groups formed and assigned a case; case-staffing format introduced
- **Due:** Genogram (group) + Weekly Reflection #1 (individual)

### Week 6 — Environmental Systems & Goal Contracting
- Ecomaps: mapping a client's environmental and systemic context (resources, stressors)
- Negotiating goals and formulating a contract: collaborative, client-centered goal-setting
- Translating assessment into a concrete, measurable treatment plan
- **Due:** Ecomap + Treatment Plan (group) + Weekly Reflection #2 (individual)

### Week 7 — Phase 1 Wrap-Up & Benchmark Checkpoint
- Synthesizing the case materials as an integrated whole
- What "ready to advance to Phase 2" means, concretely
- Benchmark Checkpoint administered
- **Due:** Phase 1 Benchmark Checkpoint (min. 80% to advance)

## Assignment: First Client Case

A semester-spanning, small-group case study running through all of Phase 1 (Weeks 5–7).

**Group product (shared):**
- Genogram (Week 5)
- Ecomap (Week 6)
- Treatment plan (Week 6)

**Individual product (each student, weekly):**
A short reflection connecting that week's case-staffing discussion to: the week's reading, the NASW Code of Ethics, EPAS Competencies, and the Afrocentric Perspective. This is not a group product — each student writes their own.

**How groups work:** Each group meets weekly ("case staffing") to discuss their assigned case using that week's reading and the group's own developing genogram/ecomap/treatment plan. Rotate who facilitates each week's staffing session. Cases are assigned from the [Case Vignette Bank](../resources/module-04-case-vignettes.md) — six fictional households varied across family structure and presenting concern, each deliberately left open rather than resolved.

**Benchmark Checkpoint (Week 7):** Scored on *both* the group's case materials (genogram, ecomap, treatment plan) and each student's individual weekly reflections. Minimum 80% required to advance to Phase 2's agency rotations. Full scoring rubric: `course-toolkit/rubrics/` (built in `module-05`).

**A note on tools:** genograms and ecomaps can be hand-drawn and scanned, or built in a diagramming tool (e.g., Lucidchart). No specialized genogram software is required.
