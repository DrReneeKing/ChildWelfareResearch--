Mental Health, Crossover Youth & the Capstone

Weeks 20–22 Mon 11/30/26, Wed 12/2/26, Finals week 12/7–11/26 Capstone · DSSWE Post-Survey

Overview

The final module of the semester. Content wraps up with two remaining population-specific topics, then the course closes with DSSWE Module 8 (Ethics, Competencies, and Professional Development), the data-science capstone, the Child Welfare Issues Paper, and the DSSWE Post-Survey — all due during finals week.

Learning Outcome

Describe the intersection of mental health needs and child welfare involvement, and the dynamics of crossover youth between child welfare and juvenile justice; apply ethical reasoning to data science practice in social work, including awareness of algorithmic bias; synthesize the semester's child welfare and data-science learning into a capstone project and written analysis.

Competency

Comp 4 (Practice-Informed Research) — formally assessed via the Child Welfare Issues Paper and Presentation. The capstone project independently demonstrates the full arc of the AUC Data Science Framework: Data Curation (working with real AFCARS data), Modeling and Mathematics & Statistics (analysis), Communication (the write-up/presentation), and Ethics (bias-aware interpretation).

Data Science Tie-In

This module closes the loop the entire semester built toward. DSSWE Module 8's ethics content — including algorithm bias in risk-assessment contexts — gives students the critical framework to interpret their own capstone findings responsibly, not just technically. The capstone itself is the first (and only) time students work directly with the restricted-use AFCARS data rather than the GCBS practice dataset, consistent with the sequencing established in resources/datasets.md. The Post-Survey revisits the same confidence/knowledge questions from the Pre-Survey (Module 03), closing the DSSWE evaluation's pre/post measurement loop.
