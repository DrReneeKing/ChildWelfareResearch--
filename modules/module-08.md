# Module 8: Evaluation, Termination, Follow-Up & Field Readiness

**Weeks:** 14–15 · **Phase:** 3 — Evaluation, Termination & Follow-Up · **GIM Stage:** Evaluation → Termination → Follow-Up

**Overview:** The course's closing module. Students synthesize the entire semester — Engagement Simulation, First Client Case, and all three Phase 2 rotations — into a Professional Portfolio, and undergo the Field Readiness Evaluation: a certification of readiness to proceed toward CUSW 404 and eventual field placement.

**Reading:** Hepworth et al., Ch. 19 (The Final Phase: Evaluation and Termination)

## Week 14 — Synthesis & Portfolio Compilation

## Week 15 — Field Readiness Certification
- What "field readiness" means concretely, and how the Field Readiness Evaluation differs from a normal graded assignment (Ready / Ready with Conditions / Not Ready)
- Final Exam
- Semester reflection and course evaluations

**Due:** Field Readiness Evaluation; Final Exam

## Assignment: Professional Portfolio & Field Readiness Evaluation

The Professional Portfolio compiles the semester's key artifacts into a single, coherent demonstration of growth across all GIM stages. The Field Readiness Evaluation is a certification (not a percentage grade) rating the student **Ready / Ready with Conditions / Not Ready** to proceed toward CUSW 404 and eventual field placement — full rubric detail is private (`course-toolkit/rubrics/`).
