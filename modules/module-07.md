# Module 7: Implementation III — Community Organizations & Gender-Sensitive Practice

**Weeks:** 12–13 · **Phase:** 2 — Implementation (agency rotations) · **GIM Stage:** Implementation

**Overview:** The third and final Phase 2 rotation, at a community organization. Pairs mezzo/macro group-work content with gender-sensitive practice, and carries the course's social justice and multiculturalism/diversity threads (Five Curricular Themes).

**Reading:** Hepworth et al. — Ch. 11 (Forming and Assessing Social Work Groups), Ch. 15 (Modifying Environments and Developing Resources)

## Week 12 — Group Work Skills; Rotation Begins
- Forming and assessing social work groups — mezzo-level skills, connected forward to the community-organization rotation's group/coalition work
- Gender-sensitive practice principles: avoiding gendered assumptions in service delivery, intersectionality with the case populations already covered (Modules 4–6)
- Community-organization rotation begins: 4 hrs/week

## Week 13 — Resource Development; Rotation Continues
- Modifying environments and developing resources — macro-level intervention content (exposure only)
- Rotation continues
- **Due:** Community asset map + reflection; Supervisor Mini-Evaluation Forms

## Assignment: Community Asset Map + Reflection

Students develop a community asset map for their rotation site's service area — identifying existing resources, gaps, and opportunities for resource development — plus a written reflection addressing social justice and intersectionality as they showed up in the rotation experience. The rotation site supervisor also completes a Supervisor Mini-Evaluation Form assessing professional conduct during the placement.
