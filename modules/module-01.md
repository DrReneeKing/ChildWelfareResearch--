# Module 1: Orientation to Generalist Practice & the GIM Model

**Week:** 1 · **Phase:** 1 — Engagement, Assessment & Planning · **GIM Stage:** — (orientation)

**Overview:** Introduces the course, the Generalist Intervention Model (GIM) as the organizing framework for the whole semester, and the Pre-Practice Experience (PPE) pilot's three-phase structure. Sets expectations before any graded practice work begins.

**Reading:** Hepworth et al., *Direct Social Work Practice*, 11th ed. — Ch. 1 (The Challenges of Social Work), Ch. 2 (Direct Practice: Domain, Philosophy, and Roles)

**Slides:** [link — TBD]

## This week
- Course introduction; syllabus walkthrough (grading, PPE phases, Benchmark Checkpoint gate, agency rotations)
- What is generalist practice? Micro, mezzo, and macro levels of intervention
- The GIM model: Engagement → Assessment → Planning → Implementation → Evaluation → Termination → Follow-Up, as the spine of this entire course
- The Pre-Practice Experience (PPE): Phase 1 (skill-building, Weeks 1–7), Phase 2 (agency rotations, Weeks 8–13), Phase 3 (portfolio & certification, Weeks 14–15)
- Preview: the CUSW 403 Practice Coach (introduced fully in Module 2)

**Due:** Orientation Quiz

## Assignment: Orientation Quiz

A short, low-stakes quiz (5% of course grade) confirming students have read the syllabus and can identify the GIM stages and PPE phase structure — not a content-mastery assessment, a readiness check before the semester's real work begins.
