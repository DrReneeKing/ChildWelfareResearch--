# Module 09: Mental Health, Crossover Youth & the Capstone

**Weeks:** 20–22 (Mon 11/30/26, Wed 12/2/26, Finals week 12/7–11/26)

## Overview

The final module of the semester. Content wraps up with two remaining population-specific topics, then the course closes with DSSWE Module 8 (Ethics, Competencies, and Professional Development), the data-science capstone, the Child Welfare Issues Paper, and the DSSWE Post-Survey — all due during finals week.

## Learning Outcome

Describe the intersection of mental health needs and child welfare involvement, and the dynamics of crossover youth between child welfare and juvenile justice; apply ethical reasoning to data science practice in social work, including awareness of algorithmic bias; synthesize the semester's child welfare and data-science learning into a capstone project and written analysis.

## Week 20 (11/30/26) — Children with Mental Health Needs in Child Welfare

- Prevalence and unique needs of children with mental health conditions in the child welfare system
- Coordinating care across child welfare and mental health service systems

## Competency

**Comp 4 (Practice-Informed Research)** — formally assessed via the Child Welfare Issues Paper and Presentation. The capstone project independently demonstrates the full arc of the AUC Data Science Framework: Data Curation (working with real AFCARS data), Modeling and Mathematics & Statistics (analysis), Communication (the write-up/presentation), and Ethics (bias-aware interpretation).

## Data Science Tie-In

This module closes the loop the entire semester built toward. DSSWE Module 8's ethics content — including algorithm bias in risk-assessment contexts — gives students the critical framework to interpret their own capstone findings responsibly, not just technically. This isn't a cold introduction: have students revisit the AI Transparency Statement and bias analysis they completed for the Ethical and Responsible Use of AI assignment back in Module 03, the same before/after pattern already used for *Kenny A.* between Modules 02 and 06. The capstone itself is the first (and only) time students work directly with the restricted-use AFCARS data rather than the GCBS practice dataset, consistent with the sequencing established in `resources/datasets.md`. The Post-Survey revisits the same confidence/knowledge questions from the Pre-Survey (Module 03), closing the DSSWE evaluation's pre/post measurement loop.
