# Module 03: Evidence-Based Practice & the Data Science Launch

**Weeks:** 5–7 (Wed 9/11/26, Fri 9/16/26 async, Wed 9/23/26 async)

## Overview

This module closes out the foundational content arc with evidence-based practice, then pivots the semester's second thread into gear: the DSSWE data-science sequence formally launches here, alongside the required DSSWE Pre-Survey.

## Learning Outcome

Explain the role of evidence-based practice in child welfare decision-making; define data science and its applications to child welfare, and demonstrate initial data science literacy.

## Week 5 (9/11/26) — Evidence-Based Practice in Child Welfare

- What "evidence-based practice" means in a child welfare context: distinguishing it from tradition-based or intuition-based practice
- How practitioners evaluate and apply research findings to individual cases
- **DSSWE Pre-Survey due** — required (ungraded) participation; see `syllabus.md` for details

## Week 6 (9/16/26, asynchronous) — Data Science Project Overview

- Introduction to the semester-long data science capstone: "Making Sense of the Numbers"
- Overview of what students will build over the semester and how it connects to child welfare practice
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 6):** 5-minute hands-on: setting a working directory in RStudio/Posit. See [Working Directory & Projects](../resources/r-rstudio-reference-guide.html#directory) in the reference guide. *Maps to the Pre/Post-Survey's working-directory knowledge-check item.*

## Week 7 (9/23/26, asynchronous) — DSSWE Module 1: Introduction to Data Science

- What is data science? Introduction to the AUC Data Science and Analytics Framework (six areas: Mathematics & Statistics, Programming, Modeling, Data Curation, Ethics, Communication)
- **Certificate: Data Science 101** — first data-science credential of the semester
- **DS Touchpoint (Week 7):** micro-demo of `install.packages()` and `library()` — loading a package, not yet using one. See [Packages](../resources/r-rstudio-reference-guide.html#packages) in the reference guide. *Maps to the Pre/Post-Survey's loading-packages knowledge-check item.*
- **Assignment: Ethical and Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Social Work** — the Ethics area of the Framework named above gets its first graded deliverable here; see `assessments/ai-ethics-assignment.md` for full instructions. Use the course-approved AI tool, [Using R for Child Welfare Research](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6756c658ff388191bffe594efd98e161-using-r-for-child-welfare-research) (a custom GPT built for this course), unless you have instructor approval to use a different one.
