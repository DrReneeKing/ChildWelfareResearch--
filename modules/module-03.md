# Module 3: Engagement & Micro Practice Skills

**Weeks:** 3–4 · **Phase:** 1 — Engagement, Assessment & Planning · **GIM Stage:** Engagement

**Overview:** The course's first applied skill-building module: micro practice skills and the interpersonal foundations of engagement, culminating in the Engagement Simulation — a recorded practice intake interview scored against the Competency 6 Engagement Rubric.

**Reading:** Hepworth et al. — Ch. 4 (Operationalizing the Cardinal Social Work Values), Ch. 5 (Relationship-Building Skills: Empathy and Authenticity), Ch. 6 (Verbal Following, Exploring, and Focusing Skills), Ch. 7 (Eliminating Counterproductive Communication Patterns)

## Week 3 — Values, Empathy & Authenticity
- Operationalizing the cardinal social work values in a real interaction, not just as abstract principles
- Relationship-building skills: what empathy sounds like in practice, and the difference between empathy and sympathy
- Authenticity in the professional relationship — genuineness without oversharing
- Engagement Simulation logistics explained: recording requirements, self-assessment component, rubric walkthrough

## Week 4 — Verbal Skills & the Engagement Simulation
- Verbal following, exploring, and focusing skills — practical technique, practiced in pairs
- Common counterproductive communication patterns (leading questions, premature reassurance, judgmental language) and how to catch yourself doing them
- **CUSW 403 Practice Coach** demo: how to use it for self-assessment feedback on your own recorded interview
- **Due:** Engagement Simulation (recorded intake interview + self-assessment)

## Assignment: Engagement Simulation

Students conduct and record a structured practice intake interview (with a classmate, friend, or family member playing a simulated client — no real clients, no real case content). Students then complete a self-assessment using the Competency 6 Engagement Rubric criteria (empathy, professionalism, cultural humility), optionally using the Practice Coach for feedback on their self-assessment (not to draft it).
