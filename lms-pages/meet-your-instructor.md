# Meet Your Instructor

I am Dr. Ivis King, your instructor for this course. My professional experience in the Human Services field spans approximately twenty-seven years. I have worked in various settings within Georgia's state government and the private sector.

While working within the [child welfare system](https://www.childwelfare.gov/), I was a social services case manager, supervisor, and Policy, Planning, and Program Development Project Administrator with the [Department of Human Services/Division of Family and Child Services (DHS/DFCS)](https://dfcs.georgia.gov/). I served as curriculum project leader; chaired a committee that developed methodology and materials for a new service model; and conducted research for child welfare program planning, implementation, evaluation, and policy development. Also, as a social services contract provider with the Department of Family and Children Services, I provided home-based services for children and families at risk of child abuse and neglect.

My program consulting experience includes grant writing for nonprofit organizations, contracting with DFCS providers, and partnering with the [Georgia State University National SafeCare Training and Research Center (NSTRC)](https://safecare.publichealth.gsu.edu/). As a certified SafeCare Trainer and Coach with NSTRC, I trained and certified masters-level social workers to be SafeCare Coaches or Home Visitors.

## Effective Teaching Environments

I have incorporated constructivism into four concepts as a framework for developing and presenting course content and materials:

1. **Evaluation** — establish the student's knowledge and skills baseline
2. **Exposure** — introduce the student to the knowledge to be learned and the skills needed
3. **Practice** — offer the student the opportunity to apply and demonstrate knowledge and skills
4. **Feedback** — document the student's progress towards mastery of knowledge and skills
