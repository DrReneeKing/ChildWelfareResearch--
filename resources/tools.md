# Tools

What students need for CUSW 403.

## Two course AI assistants — different jobs

| | Practice Coach | Course TA |
|---|---|---|
| **For** | Coaching on graded work | General course Q&A |
| **Covers** | AI Ethics Reflection, Engagement Simulation feedback, First Client Case reflections, Afrocentric Perspective Case Study | Due dates, logistics, concept explanations, rotation-site logistics, recommendation letter process, excused absence policy, AI-use policy |
| **Style** | Socratic — asks questions, never drafts submittable text | Direct — answers factual/logistics questions plainly |
| **Instructions** | [`course-ai-assistant.md`](course-ai-assistant.md) | [`course-ta-assistant.md`](course-ta-assistant.md) |

Both: never accept real client- or rotation-site-identifying information; redirect grading disputes, extensions, and accommodation requests to Dr. King directly. Deployment platform (Claude Project, Custom GPT, or other) TBD for both — the instructions are portable.

## Core tools
- **Canvas** — course site, assignment submission
- **Flipgrid** — video reflections (Afrocentric Perspective Case Study, Module 8 video reflections)
- **Zoom** (recording) — the Engagement Simulation's recorded practice intake interview (Module 3); a phone recording is also acceptable if Zoom access is a barrier for a student's practice partner

## Optional platforms, by assignment

See `course-toolkit/edtech-platform-decisions.md` for the full instructor-facing rationale, AI-ethics considerations, and institutional-setup status for each. Student-facing summary:

| Tool | Used for | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Perusall** | Weekly Hepworth readings, collaboratively annotated | Replaces the "submit a 1-page summary" pattern with shared in-text discussion |
| **Padlet** | Case-staffing group coordination; Week 1 icebreaker; Community Asset Map gallery walk | Collaborative board, no AI drafting involved |
| **Yellowdig** | Ongoing class community/discussion, in place of generic Canvas discussion boards | Requires institutional licensing — confirm before relying on it |
| **Canva** | Formatting the Community Asset Map (Module 7) or Professional Portfolio (Module 8) | Visual formatting only — the analysis and content must be the student's own |
| **Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)** | Organizing/synthesizing your own First Client Case notes or rotation observation logs across weeks | Source-grounded (summarizes what you upload) — consistent with "coach, not ghostwriter" when used this way |
| **Gamma** | Formatting a Portfolio or Asset Map presentation | Formatting only, same boundary as Canva — do not use it to generate the analytical content itself |
| **HeyGen** | *(Not recommended for graded video assignments)* | An AI avatar delivering your Engagement Simulation or Afrocentric Case Study video would replace your own recorded presence — the assignment specifically requires that presence, not a stand-in |
